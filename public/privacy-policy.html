Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 | Velarina Atelier

Velarina Atelier ("Velarina", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and personal data of our patrons. This Privacy Policy outlines our procedures concerning the collection, storage, processing, and protection of information gathered through our online boutique, located at velarina.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Velarina platform, we collect information necessary to fulfill your orders and deliver our client services:

  • Identity Data: Full name, title, and account login identifiers.
  • Contact Data: Shipping address, billing address, email address, and telephone number.
  • Transaction Data: Details of orders placed, payment confirmation tokens, item references, and order status records. We do not store raw credit card numbers on our servers; card transactions are tokenized through accredited payment processors.
  • Technical Data: Internet protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, time zone setting, operating system, and platform access logs.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing

We process your personal information strictly under lawful European and international privacy standards, including:

  • Performance of a Contract: Processing transactions, preparing courier shipments, providing order status notifications, and issuing receipts.
  • Legal Obligations: Compliance with corporate tax legislation, export documentation regulations, and statutory accounting records.
  • Legitimate Interests: Preventing unauthorized activity, protecting our network security, and refining atelier product availability.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Processors

Velarina does not sell, rent, or lease patron records to external commercial entities. Data is disclosed only to verified service partners essential for executing atelier operations:

  • Logistics providers and insured courier services for order delivery.
  • Certified payment gateway networks for transaction clearance.
  • Cloud hosting and database infrastructure providers maintaining encryption in transit and at rest.

4. Data Retention

We retain order history and transaction information for the duration mandated by applicable commercial and financial legislation (typically seven years from the transaction date). Inactive customer account data may be removed upon request.

5. Your Statutory Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable privacy laws, you possess the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we maintain concerning you.
  • Request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Request erasure of your personal data when it is no longer required for contractual or legal compliance.
  • Object to or request restriction of data processing under specific statutory circumstances.
  • Obtain data portability in a structured, commonly used format.

6. Inquiries and Contact

To exercise any of your privacy rights or submit queries regarding our data practices, please communicate directly with our privacy compliance desk:
