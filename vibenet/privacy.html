Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 18, 2026 | Version 1.0

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how VibeNet ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you visit or use our social platform and associated services.

We are committed to clear and direct privacy practices. We do not sell your personal data to third parties, nor do we run covert behavioral ad-tracking networks.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide when using VibeNet:

  • Account Information: When registering, you provide an email address, password hash (processed via Firebase Authentication), and a chosen display name.
  • Profile Information: Optional profile information you select to publish, such as a short biography or handle.
  • Content and Social Interactions: The text posts you publish, comments you submit, posts you like, and the accounts you follow.
  • Technical Metadata: System timestamps, network request headers, and client identifiers necessary to maintain connection security and real-time synchronization.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the operation and security of the platform, including:

  • Authenticating your identity and preventing unauthorized account access.
  • Transmitting and displaying your posts, comments, likes, and follower relationships in real time.
  • Enforcing our platform rules, verifying post ownership for deletion requests, and preventing spam.
  • Maintaining database integrity and diagnosing technical issues.

4. Data Storage and Backend Infrastructure

VibeNet utilizes Google Firebase (Backend as a Service), including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore:

  • Authentication: Passwords are never stored in plaintext. Passwords are salted and hashed by Google Firebase security infrastructure.
  • Firestore Database: Your user profile, posts, comments, and like records are stored in encrypted Cloud Firestore clusters managed with strict security rules.
  • Data Transmission: All data exchanged between your browser and VibeNet is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS protocols.

5. Your Rights and Control

You retain direct control over your content and account:

  • Editing Profile: You may update your display name and biography at any time from your profile dashboard.
  • Content Deletion: You have the permanent right to delete any post you have authored. Deleting a post removes it from the public feed.
  • Account Erasure: You can submit a deletion request to purge your account records by contacting our administration.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

VibeNet uses local storage and standard session tokens strictly for authentication persistence and interface state. We do not employ third-party cross-site marketing cookies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services evolve. Any updates will be reflected directly on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Information

For questions or requests concerning your privacy, contact the VibeNet engineering and administrative team at: privacy@vibenet.network.
