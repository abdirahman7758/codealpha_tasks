Material Integrity

Vegetable-Tanned Tuscan Calfskin

Unlike chemically chrome-tanned leathers, our Tuscan calfskin is treated using natural tannins derived from chestnut and mimosa bark. Over time, the leather oxidizes and absorbs natural oils, developing an amber patina distinct to each owner.

All edges are bevelled by hand and sealed with raw organic beeswax to preserve breathability and moisture resistance.
